Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

Digital credits

Topper credits are digital service minutes added after confirmed payment. Before Start Listening, unused credits remain in your account.

Eligible refund requests

Contact us within 7 days if you were charged more than once for the same intended purchase, payment was captured but credits were not delivered, or a verified technical failure prevented use of the purchased credits.

Consumed credits

Minutes already consumed are normally non-refundable. Approved refunds may be reduced by the value of consumed credits and applicable non-refundable payment charges, where legally permitted.

Failed or pending payments

Failed payments do not receive credits. Amounts temporarily debited for failed or pending payments are normally reversed by the bank or payment provider according to their processing timelines.

How to request

Email support.topper@gmail.com with your registered email, Razorpay payment ID, payment date and issue description. Do not send card details, CVV, UPI PIN or banking passwords.

Processing

Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method. Final credit timing depends on Razorpay, the card network, UPI provider and your bank.
