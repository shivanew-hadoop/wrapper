Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Digital service delivery

Topper interview credits are digital service minutes credited to the registered account after confirmed payment. There is no physical shipping. Unused credits remain on the account until consumed or otherwise adjusted under the service terms.

Eligible refund requests

Contact support within 7 days of purchase if you were charged more than once for the same intended purchase, payment was captured but the purchased credits were not delivered, or a verified Topper service failure prevented meaningful use of the purchased credits.

Consumed credits

Minutes already consumed are normally non-refundable. Where a partial refund is approved and legally permitted, the refundable amount may reflect unused service value and any non-refundable payment charges imposed by the payment provider.

Failed, pending or reversed payments

Failed payments do not receive credits. Amounts temporarily debited for failed or pending payments are normally reversed by the bank or payment provider according to their processing timelines. If a payment remains unresolved, contact support with the order reference.

Cancellation

Topper currently sells one-time digital credit packs rather than an auto-renewing subscription, so there is no recurring plan to cancel. You can simply choose not to purchase additional credits.

How to request a refund

Email support.topper@gmail.com with your registered email, payment/order ID, payment date and a short description of the issue. Never send your password, full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking password.

Processing

Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment gateway, card network, UPI provider and your bank.
