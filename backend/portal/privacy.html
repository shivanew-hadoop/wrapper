Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

Information we process

We process account information such as name, email, password hash, credit balance and transaction history. During use, Topper processes interview context, audio-derived text, typed prompts and screen captures you explicitly request.

Purpose

Information is used to authenticate accounts, process payments, provide AI responses, meter credits, prevent abuse, support users and improve reliability.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Topper does not collect or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or bank credentials. We retain limited order identifiers, status, amount and timestamps for reconciliation.

Service providers

Topper uses infrastructure, payment, transcription and AI providers only as needed to deliver the service. Their processing is governed by their respective terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

Account and transaction records are retained for operational, fraud-prevention and legal purposes. We use access controls, signed sessions, encrypted transport and one-time desktop launch tokens, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request account information correction or account deletion, subject to legally required transaction retention, by emailing support.topper@gmail.com.

Contact

Privacy questions: support.topper@gmail.com.
