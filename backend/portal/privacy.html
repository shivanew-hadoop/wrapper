LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Information we process

We process account information such as name, email, password hash, credit balance and transaction history. When you use the desktop application, Topper may process candidate documents you upload, audio-derived text, typed prompts, generated responses and screen captures you explicitly request.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, prepare interview context, provide AI-assisted responses, meter credits, process and reconcile payments, maintain recent transcript history, prevent abuse and support users.

Payments

Payments are handled by a supported third-party payment gateway, currently PhonePe where configured. Topper does not ask you to provide card CVV, UPI PIN or online-banking passwords. We retain limited order identifiers, amount, status and timestamps needed for reconciliation and support.

Service providers

Topper uses infrastructure, payment, transcription and AI providers only as needed to operate the service. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention

Account and transaction records may be retained for operational, fraud-prevention, support and legal purposes. Recent interview transcript history is limited by the product's configured retention behavior. You should avoid uploading information you are not authorized to process.

Security

Topper uses access controls, signed sessions, encrypted transport and one-time desktop launch tokens where supported. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of account information, subject to transaction or other records that must be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons, by contacting support.topper@gmail.com.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support.topper@gmail.com.
