ABOUT US

Topper

Topper is a Windows-based AI interview workspace that helps users prepare candidate context, convert supported live audio into text, add user-requested screen context and receive AI-assisted responses during a session. The service is delivered digitally through a registered Topper account.

What we provide

Topper provides account access, interview-time credits, the Windows desktop application, resume-first context preparation, optional job-description context, live transcription support, AI-assisted responses, screen-context prompts and recent session transcript access.
