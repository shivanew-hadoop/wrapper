PAYMENTS

Delivery & Fulfilment Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

1. Digital delivery only

Topper provides a digital software service and digital interview-time credits. No physical goods are shipped and no courier or postal delivery is involved.

2. When credits are delivered

After the payment gateway confirms a successful payment, the purchased credits are added electronically to the registered Topper account. In normal operation this happens automatically within a few minutes of payment confirmation.

3. Access to the service

Users can view the updated credit balance in the account portal and launch or download the Windows desktop application from the same account. Credits are associated with the registered account and are not delivered as a physical voucher.

4. Delayed or missing delivery

If payment is shown as successful but credits have not appeared within 30 minutes, contact support.topper@gmail.com with the registered email, payment/order ID and payment date. We will verify the server-side payment status before applying any account correction.

5. Failed or pending payments

Credits are not delivered for failed payments. Pending payments remain subject to payment-gateway or bank confirmation. Amounts debited for transactions that later fail or reverse are handled according to the payment provider and issuing bank timelines.

6. Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility and processing are governed by the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
